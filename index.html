04 / The thesis

Artists are an asset class

Most labels sign hits. Million Crowns co-invests in artists, early, and builds value that compounds.

We are not just an independent label. We are the label of the future. Every artist we back is a unique asset class, generating income from streaming, publishing, touring, licensing, brand partnerships, and more. Each stream opens new investment opportunities to create, distribute, and grow, and that value compounds over a career, not a single release.

We act as co-investors alongside our artists, putting capital and infrastructure behind them from the very beginning, with one goal: to help them influence culture and society through their art.

Our edge is the combination of technology and cultural influence. We use data, digital tools, and our networks across music, media, and tech to put each artist in front of the right audiences and push their reach as far as it can go.

  • Co-invest from the very beginning Seed
  • Build across every income stream Build
  • Scale reach with tech and cultural influence Scale
  • Compound value and expand into culture Grow

05 / Network

Who we build with

Our artists get access to the rooms that move careers: label executives, major labels, platinum producers, and the studios where classics were made.

Million Crowns works alongside executives and partners at major labels across Los Angeles, Atlanta, Houston, and Chicago. We open those relationships to every artist we invest in, so the right deal, the right producer, and the right audience arrive at the right time.

Our artists record in major studios across the country, including legendary rooms like the Record Plant in Los Angeles, where Hotel California, Rumours, and The College Dropout were made, and SugarHill Recording Studios in Houston, the oldest continuously operating recording studio in the United States, along with ATS Records. In those rooms they work side by side with the producers and engineers who shaped the sound of past generations and are shaping the sound of the next one.

06 / Team

Co-Founders & Managing Partners

KM
Co-Founder & Managing Partner

Kelson "Rawk" Miller

Rawk is a Los Angeles music executive who has been part of the LA and Chicago scenes for years. He has worked with everyone from OZ ON THE TRACK, Donn Robb, and Grammy-winning producer CBMIX (Chris Barnett) to international acts, and built a network of studios, producers, and label executives that he spends on the artists he believes in. He founded Million Crowns Music, where he leads management and A&R, and Rawket House Creative.

This is what Rawk does. He brings artists into major studios like the Record Plant, puts up the capital and the relationships to launch their careers, and connects them with the people who move the industry. He turned that playbook into a label. Rawk sets the creative standard and opens the doors.

JA
Co-Founder & Managing Partner

Jodi Anderson Jr.

Jodi brings the investment model and the technology. He co-founded Rézme, a venture-backed technology company, during his time at Stanford University, where he earned a BA and an MA and worked at the Stanford Accelerator for Learning. He has also worked at Reddit, previously directed technological innovation at Cornell University, and at CreateSafe served on the management team for Grimes, HANA, Take A Daytrip, BloodPop, and several other pop acts.

At Million Crowns he runs the label like a venture portfolio: backing artists early, building the operating systems around them, and using tech to grow their reach.
